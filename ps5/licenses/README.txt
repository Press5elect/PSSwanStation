source at the revision BUILD.txt names, plus the ps5 folder; components.json
in this folder names every part's source and the revision it was built from,
and a release attaches an archive of each. LEGAL.txt beside eboot.bin says
what the title may be used with.

This title contains no games, no BIOS file of an original console, no console
firmware and no keys. Use games you own, from your own discs, and a BIOS
dumped from hardware you own.
