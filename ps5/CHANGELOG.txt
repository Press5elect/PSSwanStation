PSSwanStation - what changed
============================

Build 9
  - Library: L2 and R2 jump to the previous and the next letter.
  - Search: press the touch pad in the library, type a part of the name, and
    the games from the console, USB and the network that match are listed.
  - Menu sounds (Settings, Interface, "Interface sounds" to switch them off).

Build 8
  - Opening Options or Cheats for a network game no longer freezes the
    screen while the share answers.
  - Loading a network game: one progress bar for the whole game, the swan no
    longer jumps back, the text is under the bar with speed and time left.

Build 7
  - The title is now called PSSwanStation.

Build 6
  - Games from an FTP server (a "path = ftp://..." line in network.cfg).
  - Players 2 to 4: other users logged in on the console, picked up while
    the title runs; the multitap is on the Controllers page.

Build 5
  - New start-up animation with sound: the swan takes the lift.

Build 4
  - The library only shows the places (Internal, USB, Network) that have games.

Build 3
  - The swan moves: it flies to its corner, looks about, flies at the screen.

Build 2
  - Start-up screen, a game's details with description, its own options and
    its cheats, disc change from the details, playlists hidden.
